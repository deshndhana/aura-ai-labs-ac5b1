Our Story

Born from a Deep Love
for This Resplendent Isle

Aura Lanka Expeditions was founded in 2013 by a passionate team of Sri Lankan travel professionals who believed the world deserved to experience their homeland beyond its beaches — through its jungle temples, living villages, ancient wisdom, and warm-hearted people.

Today, we are recognized as one of Sri Lanka's most innovative destination management companies, holding full accreditation from the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA). Every journey we design is carbon-offset, community-funded, and crafted with respect for both the traveler and the land.
