Our Philosophy

Architecture as a living act of intention.

"We do not build for the present. We build for the generations who will inherit the spaces we conceive — and we take that responsibility seriously."

— Erik Sørensen, Principal Architect

Founded in Oslo in 2009, Aura Lines Architecture has grown from a boutique design atelier into an internationally recognised studio with studios in Dubai, Tokyo, and São Paulo. Our portfolio spans private residences, cultural institutions, luxury hospitality, and master-planned communities across four continents.

Our methodology is rooted in place — in the geology of a site, the quality of its light, the culture that surrounds it. We refuse the language of trend. Instead, we pursue an architecture that is simultaneously of its moment and wholly resistant to obsolescence.

Sustainability is not a feature layer; it is the structural premise of every decision we make. From passive solar orientation and rainwater harvesting to biophilic material palettes, responsible design is built into our practice at every scale.

Core Disciplines

Three disciplines.
One vision.

Each project we undertake is guided by a holistic understanding of space — architectural structure, interior experience, and landscape context are never treated in isolation. They are a single, inseparable act of design.

From conceptual master plans to construction documentation, our architectural design service encompasses the full spectrum of built form. We work with private clients, developers, and cultural institutions to produce buildings that are structurally resolute and spatially generous.

Every commission begins with an extended period of site analysis, client dialogue, and programme definition. We believe the design process should be as considered as the finished building itself.

Scope Includes

  • Concept Design & Feasibility
  • Schematic & Design Development
  • Planning & Permit Documentation
  • Technical Construction Drawings
  • Site Supervision & Contract Administration

Interior space, when curated with precision, becomes the most intimate form of architecture. Our interior team approaches each project as a material and sensory composition — an exercise in restraint, texture, and lived quality.

We work across residential, hospitality, and commercial typologies, sourcing artisan furniture, custom joinery, and collectible objects that carry a depth of craft and provenance rarely found in conventional specification.

Scope Includes

  • Spatial Planning & Mood Direction
  • Material & Finish Specification
  • Custom Joinery & Furniture Design
  • Lighting Design & Art Curation
  • FF&E Procurement & Installation

A building without considered landscape is incomplete. Our landscape practice designs exterior environments that extend the logic and material language of the architecture — from intimate private gardens to large-scale ground-plane strategies for commercial developments.

We work closely with ecologists, horticulturalists, and water engineers to ensure every planted environment is biodiverse, low-maintenance, and climatically appropriate.

Scope Includes

  • Masterplan & Ground Strategy
  • Planting Design & Species Selection
  • Water Feature & Pool Integration
  • Hardscape & Paving Specification
  • Environmental & Biodiversity Reporting
