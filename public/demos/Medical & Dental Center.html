About AuraCare

Where Clinical Excellence Meets Human Compassion

Founded in 2006, AuraCare Medical & Dental Center has grown from a single Colombo clinic to a network of three state-of-the-art facilities, serving over 95,000 patients across Sri Lanka. We hold full accreditation from the Sri Lanka Medical Council and maintain international standards of sterility, diagnostic accuracy, and patient privacy.

Our multi-disciplinary team of 42 specialist physicians works collaboratively — ensuring that every patient receives a holistic, coordinated care plan that addresses both immediate health concerns and long-term wellness goals.
