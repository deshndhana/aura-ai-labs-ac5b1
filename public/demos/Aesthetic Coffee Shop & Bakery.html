Our Story

Rooted in craft,
poured with purpose.

The Daily Grind was born from a simple obsession: finding the world's most extraordinary coffee and sharing it, cup by honest cup, with our community. In 2018, founders Maya & Elliot traded their corporate desks for a 400-square-foot roastery in East Nashville and never looked back.

Every origin we source — from the highlands of Ethiopia's Yirgacheffe to the volcanic soils of Guatemala Antigua — is the result of direct relationships with smallholder farmers paid above fair-trade premiums. Our pastry team arrives at 4 AM so that when you walk through our door, the croissants are still warm.
