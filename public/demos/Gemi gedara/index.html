Our Story

ගෙදර රසෙයි
The Taste of Home

Welcome to ගෙමිගෙදර හෙළ රස — where every dish carries the soul of Sri Lanka. Born from a passion for authentic village cooking, we bring you the timeless flavours of traditional rice & curry, sizzling hot kottu, and crispy short eats.

Our recipes are passed down through generations — using fresh coconut milk, hand-ground spice mixes, and the finest local ingredients sourced from village markets every morning.

We believe food is more than nourishment — it is memory, culture, and love. Every plate we serve is made with the same care as a mother cooking for her family.
