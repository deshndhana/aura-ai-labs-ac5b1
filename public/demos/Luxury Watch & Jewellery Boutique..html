Bespoke Service

The Art of Making
Something Singular

Our Bespoke Programme invites you into the atelier — not as a customer, but as a collaborator. Guided by a dedicated Creative Consultant and Master Craftsman, you will select from our reserve of ethically certified diamonds, Burmese rubies, Colombian emeralds, and Kashmir sapphires.

Your chosen stone is then hand-set into a mounting sculpted from 18-carat hand-rolled gold, shaped using tools unchanged since 1924. The result is not merely jewellery — it is autobiography.

Exclusive access to our reserve of GIA-certified, ethically sourced gemstones and precious alloys.

III

Delivery of your completed piece, accompanied by its provenance documentation and lifelong care covenant.
