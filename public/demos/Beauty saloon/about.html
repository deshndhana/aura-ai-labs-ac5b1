A Decade of Beauty Mastery

Lumière was born from a simple belief: every person deserves to feel extraordinary. Founded in 2012, we've built a sanctuary where art meets beauty, and every client is treated as the individual they are.

Our team of master stylists and beauty therapists have trained at some of the world's finest academies, bringing international expertise to every appointment. We're passionate about continuous learning, regularly attending global industry events and training programs.

We use only the finest, ethically sourced products — because we believe luxury and responsibility go hand in hand.
