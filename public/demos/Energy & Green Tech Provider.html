The Process

From Consultation
to Clean Power in 4 Steps

Our streamlined installation process means you go from initial enquiry to generating your own electricity in as little as 3 weeks.

1

Free Site Assessment

Our certified energy consultant visits your property, analyses roof orientation, shading, and your usage patterns — completely free, no commitment required.

Custom Engineering
