Our Story

Tattoo is a Sacred Art Form

Founded in 2014 by master artists Elena Voss and Marcus Kaine, INKBLOOD Studio was born from a shared obsession — the belief that skin is the most intimate canvas a human being possesses.

We don't just tattoo. We listen, we collaborate, we craft. Every piece that leaves our studio carries the weight of intention, the precision of technique, and the soul of genuine artistry. No flash. No rush. No compromises.

Our philosophy is simple: your story deserves to be told beautifully, permanently, and fearlessly.

What We Do

Tattoo Styles

From ancient tribal motifs to hyper-realistic portraiture — we command every language that ink can speak.

01
Traditional
Bold lines, vibrant fills, classic motifs. The cornerstone of Western tattoo heritage — anchors, eagles, roses, and daggers rendered with unwavering confidence.
02
Tribal
Rooted in ancient cultures from Polynesia, Maori, and beyond. Geometric power patterns that carry deep ancestral meaning and raw visual strength.
03
Neo-Traditional
Where old-school meets modern. Ornate line work, rich color palettes, and detailed shading elevate classic imagery into contemporary masterpieces.
04
Realistic
Photographic precision translated into skin. Portraits, animals, nature scenes — rendered with flawless grayscale or full color that defies belief.
05
Watercolor
The painter's brushstroke made permanent. Soft bleeds, vibrant splashes, and dreamlike gradients that look like living art drying on your skin.
