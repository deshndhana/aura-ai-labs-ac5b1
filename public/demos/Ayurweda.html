Healing Arts Pavilion

Rooted in 5,000 years of Ayurvedic philosophy, our healing sanctuary offers a holistic journey for mind, body, and spirit. Under the guidance of Sri Lanka's most revered Ayurvedic physicians, each treatment is personally curated to restore your innate balance.

Panchakarma Detox Retreats

Our signature 3, 5, and 7-night purification therapies supervised by certified Panchakarma specialists using organically sourced medicinal oils and herbs.

Vedic Sound & Energy Therapy

Tibetan singing bowls, Nada Yoga, and Chakra balancing sessions conducted in our open-air teak pavilions overlooking Weligama Bay.

Begin with an in-depth Prakriti (body constitution) analysis and receive a bespoke plan incorporating diet, herbal medicine, yoga, and targeted therapies.
